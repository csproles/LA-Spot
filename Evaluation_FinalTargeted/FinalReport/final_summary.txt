11. LIMITATIONS
------------------
- Sensitivity remains well under 100% even for V3 (57.2% on locked test) —
  this is a visual-concern screening aid, not a diagnostic tool, and should
  never be presented as one.
- Precision is low (30-36%) at every configuration tested — most
  "elevated concern" flags will be false alarms on this cohort composition.
- patient_id is missing for over half the cohort; the singleton fallback is
  a reasonable, documented compromise, not a guarantee of zero leakage for
  those rows.
- D_mm remains unavailable (no physical calibration); D_px only.
- No internal color/pigment-asymmetry measurement exists; A is silhouette-
  only, a documented, deliberate scope decision, not a bug.
- V3 was selected and frozen before the locked test was run and was not
  retuned afterward, per the project's own rule — its real-world
  generalization beyond this dataset is untested.

This is a research/competition-prototype evaluation on a public research
dataset. It is not clinically validated and does not diagnose melanoma.
