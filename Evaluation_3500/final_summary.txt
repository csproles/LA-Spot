7. NOTABLE ANOMALIES / LIMITATIONS SURFACED AT THIS SCALE
--------------------------------
- large_area_fraction flag rate jumped from ~5% (43-image test set) to 25.9%
  here. Not something to act on within this fixed evaluation run, but a strong
  signal that oversized-mask segmentation behavior is far more common at scale
  than the small test set suggested, and needs its own investigation before
  any future run.
- low_confidence (<0.5) rate is 30.5%, also much higher than the small test
  set. Mean/median confidence (0.63/0.65) is noticeably lower than typically
  seen on the smaller, more curated demo dataset.
- clin_size_long_diam_mm exists in this larger dataset's metadata (23% of all
  8277 images) even though it does not exist in the smaller project dataset
  used for all prior steps. It was NOT used here, per the fixed, unmodified
  D_mm=None policy — flagged for future consideration only.
- No FAILED rows at all across all 3500 images — the pipeline's error handling
  was never exercised by a real failure in this run; robustness under actual
  failure conditions remains unverified beyond the deliberate try/except design.
