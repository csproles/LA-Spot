EVALUATION_3500 — WHY DOES A FAIL TO TRIGGER ON MELANOMA FALSE NEGATIVES?
============================================================================
Read-only mechanism investigation. Reproduces revised_abcd/revised_asymmetry.py
(the code that actually produced every saved A_value/A_concern in
Evaluation_3500) step by step, verified against the saved results: all 20
reproductions matched the saved A_value exactly (0 mismatches). No YOLO,
ABCD, threshold, mask, or scoring code was modified or rerun.

Selection: 10 melanoma FN and 10 melanoma TP, both restricted to cases
WITHOUT the large_area_fraction flag, fixed seed 20260918.

Selected FN IDs: ISIC_0406149, ISIC_3210975, ISIC_4552372, ISIC_4953281,
  ISIC_4311112, ISIC_9111814, ISIC_4874581, ISIC_2867998, ISIC_9957368,
  ISIC_1887299
Selected TP IDs: ISIC_9276896, ISIC_7801345, ISIC_1220536, ISIC_2149878,
  ISIC_7454892, ISIC_2717192, ISIC_0866872, ISIC_4203842, ISIC_3486312,
  ISIC_8899125

All 20 panels (7 images + full numeric readout each) are in panels/, and
every intermediate value is in asymmetry_investigation_manifest.csv.

- All 10 FN masks have BOTH horizontal and vertical IoU above 0.777 — every
  single one. None come close to the 0.20 concern threshold on A (the
  highest FN value was 0.186).
- TP masks are both lower on average AND far more spread out (h-IoU down to

  other 2 (A=0.185, A=0.179) do NOT — those two TP outcomes were driven by
  something other than A (B and/or C, or the critical-color override), not
  by asymmetry.
- Visually (see panels), the 10 FN masks are consistently round-to-oval,
  gently and fairly evenly scalloped shapes — no single dominant protrusion
  or lopsided extension. Their overlap/disagreement panels are dominated by
  green (agreement), with only thin, evenly-distributed red/blue slivers at
  the scalloped edges.
- Visually, the TP masks consistently show one or more distinct, uneven
  protrusions, "legs," or lopsided bulges without a mirrored counterpart —
  their overlap/disagreement panels show large, concentrated red/blue
  regions at specific spots, not a thin even rim.
- PCA alignment does not appear to be distorting these particular masks:
  none of the 20 raw masks are strongly elongated to begin with, so the
  rotation step barely changes their apparent shape in panel 3 versus panel
  2. The alignment step is not observed to be "hiding" asymmetry in this
  sample — the masks look about equally round/irregular before and after
  alignment.

ANSWERS TO THE SPECIFIC QUESTIONS ASKED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Do FN masks genuinely appear relatively symmetric?
A: Yes, consistently, in this sample of 10 — both by eye and by IoU. This is
   a direct observation, not a hypothesis: every FN mask examined has a
   compact, round-ish outline with only mild, evenly-distributed edge
   texture.

Q: Does PCA alignment make an asymmetric lesion appear more symmetric?
A: Not observed in this sample. None of the 20 selected masks are elongated
   enough for alignment to matter much — alignment mainly changes outcomes
   for elongated shapes (as shown in the earlier investigation/
   investigate_asymmetry.py work), and these particular FN masks aren't
   elongated to begin with. This does not rule out alignment mattering for
   OTHER melanoma cases not in this 20-case sample; it simply wasn't
   observed here.

Q: Is horizontal/vertical IoU insensitive to important irregularities?
A: Partially, and this is where observation gives way to hypothesis.
   Directly observed: IoU clearly DOES respond to large, uneven protrusions
   (the TP cases prove this — real lopsided bulges drove IoU down to
   0.53-0.71). Hypothesis, not directly tested here: IoU-on-a-silhouette can
   only ever measure asymmetry of SHAPE. It has no way to detect asymmetry
   of color or internal structure (e.g. pigment concentrated on one side of
   an otherwise round lesion) because it never looks inside the mask, only
   at its outline. Since the clinical ABCD "asymmetry" concept traditionally
   includes asymmetry of color/structure as well as shape, a shape-only
   measure would systematically miss that category of case. This analysis
   did not examine internal color distribution, so this remains a
   hypothesis for a possible follow-up, not a confirmed finding.
   Also a secondary, directly observed pattern: IoU is forgiving of mild,
   evenly-spread scalloping (a bumpy-but-round edge, like a gear or flower
   shape) because the bumps roughly mirror each other in aggregate — this
   is visually distinct from a single large uneven protrusion, and the
   current calculation cannot and does not try to distinguish "evenly
   textured" from "genuinely smooth," only whether the OVERALL silhouette
   folds onto itself.

Q: Why do good-looking FN masks frequently produce A<0.20?
A: Direct observation: because their silhouettes genuinely are close to
   round/compact by the numbers (IoU consistently >=0.777) as well as by
   eye. The calculation is behaving as designed on the mask it was given.
   Whether the ORIGINAL LESION is truly "not asymmetric" by a dermatologist's
   full clinical definition (which can include color/structure asymmetry
   this measure cannot see) is a separate question this analysis does not
   answer.

Q: What differs geometrically from TP masks where A>0.20?
A: Direct observation: TP masks have one or more distinct, uneven
   protrusions or extensions without a mirrored counterpart, visible both by
   eye and in the lower/more-variable IoU values. This is the geometric
   difference the calculation is actually detecting.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
This 20-case sample only covers melanoma FN/TP WITHOUT the large_area_fraction
flag, per instruction. It says nothing about the large_area_fraction FN
cases (already separately visually reviewed by the user, with mixed
results — some genuinely poor masks, some reasonable ones). It also does
not test the color/internal-structure-asymmetry hypothesis above; that
would require a different kind of measurement than what this script (or the
existing A calculation) computes. No alternative asymmetry algorithm was
implemented or is being proposed here.

No thresholds were changed, tuned, or recommended based on this analysis.
